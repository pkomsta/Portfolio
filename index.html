04 — Background

About

I'm a Unity developer. Most of my commercial work has been client-side — gameplay, UI/frontend, and the integration layer that connects a game to its backend — but I've spent enough time on the server side (MongoDB, Docker, Grafana) to be comfortable owning a feature end to end.

The part I enjoy most is tooling. Custom UPM packages and Unity Editor extensions are quiet multipliers: they make the whole team faster and keep asset pipelines from turning into a mess. I also lean on AI tools for rapid mechanic prototyping and test support, which shortens the loop between an idea and something playable.
